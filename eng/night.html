FAQ

Do I need to buy tickets for Prance The Night?

+

Prance The Night is included in the pass. By purchasing the pass, you can participate in Prance The Night without the need to buy additional tickets

What is the schedule for Prance The Night?

+

Prance The Night is usually held on the evening of Day 1. For the exact time, please notice the announcements

I have a history of photosensitive epilepsy. Can I participate?

+

For the sake of your health, we suggest that you consult a doctor before participating to confirm whether there are any related risks
