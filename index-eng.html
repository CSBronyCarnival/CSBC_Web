FAQ

Is there any difference in the activity on the 18th/19th?

+

Yeeeeeeeeeeeeeep!
The events on the 18th and 19th are not the same, please enjoy them!

How to receive my con pass and stuff?

+

They will be granted at the venue on convention days.
Please show the order to our staffs to get your pass and rewards.

Are there any entry and exit restrictions during the convention?

+

There are no restrictions. Attendees can freely enter and exit the venue with their pass during the convention.
The pass is included in your rewards. This is the only proof of entry, please do not lose it.

Is there a separate official hotel available for booking?

+

The hotel room is not only available in 3-DAY HOTEL ROOM WITH 2-DAY TICKET.
You can also make a separate reservation by asking staffs.

Is there a limit on the number of tickets?

+

The total number of our ticket is currently not limited. Different types of tickets have limitations and different quantities.
For more information, please follow our offical updates.

What should I do if I need to change the room information?

+

Please contact staffs to change the information, whether it's about your check-in time or have other issues.
