About Us

We are CSBC, founded in Guangzhou. Since 2022, we have successfully held multiple convention in the southern region of China.
We are committed to working with Bronies to think together, innovate together, and progress together in the process of development, bringing you a better convention experience.

How to receive my crowdfunding rewards?

+

Our crowdfunding feedback will be grant at the exhibition site.
You can show the ticket purchase order page to the ticket inspector to get your rewards.

+

There are no restrictions. Visitors can freely enter and exit the venue with their tickets during the exhibition period.
The ticket is included in your crowdfunding feedback. This is the only proof of entry, please do not lose it.
